The weather widget and the shortcut (webhook) tiles are removed. Home no longer shows the weather or a prompt to set a location, Settings → Widgets now lists Date, Greeting and Inputs, and the phone or computer shortcut editor is gone. Astro no longer contacts Open-Meteo and never sends shortcut requests. Your other settings are kept; saved weather locations, temperature units and shortcut tiles are discarded the next time preferences are saved, and the stored forecast cache is deleted when Astro starts.
