Rename the launcher to Astro. The app now uses the ID `com.danielcanoh22.astro`, the `astro` executable, the `astro-helper` TV helper and `astro-home` paths on the TV, so it installs as a new app beside the old `com.ecklf.luma` installation instead of updating it. Before removing the old install, disable its Home takeover and its TV input; Astro hides the old app from its inventory. Preferences are not migrated automatically.
